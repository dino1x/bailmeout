## 3. Production & Recording Guide for X

### Framing & Visual Setup
- **Screen Recording:** Record your iPhone screen directly using iOS Screen Recording (`Settings > Control Center > Screen Recording`).
- **Camera Footage:** Use a secondary phone or webcam to record your reaction / hands under the table for the first 5 seconds. This human element stops the scroll on X much faster than a pure screen recording.
- **Audio Capture:** When playing the voice memo and taking the Twilio call, make sure your microphone records the speaker audio crisp and clear with zero echo.

### Editing Checklist (CapCut, Premiere, or Final Cut)
1. **Pacing:** Cut out all dead air. The moment the heart reaction is tapped, the text and audio bubble should appear within 1-2 seconds (speed up wait time in edit if needed).
2. **Auto-Captions:** Generate bold, dynamic captions (white text on dark background, or yellow highlights) synchronized to your voice.
3. **Sound Effects:** Add subtle whoosh or pop sounds when bubbles arrive to keep viewer attention locked.
