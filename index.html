Frequently Asked Questions

Can anyone tell that BailMeOut is an automated bot?

No. The message bubbles appear in authentic native iMessage blue. The audio memos are native voice notes recorded with human-like breathing and background noise. The phone call arrives through real cellular telephone networks from an actual telephone number.

How do I trigger an escape if my phone is on the table?

Simply double-tap any message in your BailMeOut iMessage thread and tap the Heart reaction icon. You do not need to type, unlock a special app, or look down.

Does my computer need to stay awake or turned on?

No. BailMeOut is deployed as an autonomous 24/7 background worker in the cloud (Railway / Render / Docker). Your alibi agent functions continuously even if your laptop is completely powered off.

What is the Dead-Man's Switch feature?

Text "bail in 30m" before heading into a date or meeting. If you haven't dismissed the check-in text, BailMeOut automatically fires the full extraction cascade at the designated minute.
