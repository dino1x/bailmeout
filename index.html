Technical Primitives

Full-Stack Cloud Architecture

Built on carrier-grade gRPC messaging, resilient HTTP dispatchers, and containerized cloud workers.

PS

Photon Spectrum

Maintains an Apple iMessage carrier gateway. Detects incoming reactions, handles tapbacks natively, and delivers instant bidirectional Apple messages over gRPC.

11L

ElevenLabs Voice AI

Synthesizes high-fidelity voice notes with emotional tone modulation, stress inflections, and native Apple M4A container encoding for authentic speaker playback.

TW

Twilio Voice & TwiML

Places automated outbound PSTN phone calls directly to your device via Polly speech engines. Twimlet endpoints guarantee immediate audible delivery upon answering.
